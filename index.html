Our Story

Built on experience
and family values

Founded in 1990 by Gaurav Chaubey, AVNI Infrastructure has grown as a family business with a focus on real estate and construction.

The next generation brings a modern technical perspective to the business. Anvi Chaubey combines civil engineering, construction management, project management and sustainable construction knowledge with hands-on business experience.

Our approach connects design thinking, engineering discipline, responsible planning and client-focused execution across India and Canada.
